📌 About this System

This Flood Risk Prediction System is a Machine Learning-based web application developed to estimate rainfall-induced flood risk in Tamil Nadu. The system uses three important parameters: District, Month and Total Rainfall. Three machine learning models are used in the prediction process: Random Forest, Decision Tree and Logistic Regression. The predictions from all three models are combined using a majority voting approach to generate the final flood-risk result. The system also provides safety recommendations to help users take preventive measures during periods of heavy rainfall.
