📌 About this System

This Flood Risk Prediction System is a Machine Learning-based web application developed to estimate the possibility of flood occurrence in Tamil Nadu. The prediction is based on three important parameters: District, Month and Total Rainfall. A trained Random Forest machine learning model analyzes these inputs and predicts whether the selected area has a High Flood Risk or Low Flood Risk. The system also provides safety recommendations to help users take preventive measures during heavy rainfall.
